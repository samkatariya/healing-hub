# Simplify Healing Emotions without losing its character

## Direction
Keep the current page structure and calm, spacious feel. Make the site easier to scan, less repetitive, and less insistent about contacting us. Use the logo's blue as the main accent, with a restrained sage green as a supporting colour; avoid a loud two-colour treatment. Use Libre Baskerville for headings and IBM Plex Sans for body text, as chosen.

## Visitor-facing changes
1. **Make the homepage shorter and more purposeful.** Keep a clear introduction, a compact overview of who/what Healing Emotions supports, a concise trust section, and a simple route to contact. Move detailed lists of specialties, professionals, locations, organizations, and first-session information to their existing pages. Remove repetitive teasers, placeholder-style gallery blocks, and duplicated calls to action.
2. **Quiet the contact prompts.** Keep one obvious booking action in the header and a clear path on the Contact page; use at most one contextual action where a page genuinely needs one. Remove repeated WhatsApp links and booking prompts from every item and page section. Preserve the working booking flow and direct contact options for visitors who choose them.
3. **Edit copy across public pages for clarity.** Replace stacked claims, jargon, and promotional filler with short, human descriptions. Keep the actual services, fees, locations, and organizational offerings discoverable on their dedicated pages. Avoid adding unverified claims or invented client stories; only display testimonials that have been confirmed for public use.
4. **Reduce navigation density.** Keep the essential destinations visible and let secondary pages remain accessible from relevant pages and the footer. Simplify the footer so it serves as navigation and contact information, not another pitch.
5. **Refine the visual system.** Replace the heavy solid-brown opening with a calmer image-led introduction using an existing relevant photograph, keeping the text legible and the next section visible. Apply a restrained blue-and-sage palette through shared colour tokens; keep section layouts familiar rather than introducing a new visual language. Make the choice areas more clearly interactive without boxing the whole site into cards.
6. **Repair known presentation issues.** Use the uploaded original logo instead of the broken asset link, refresh the favicon from the same mark, and fix the missing arrow-icon imports on Fees and Locations. Check other public pages for the same kind of failure.

## Technical notes
- Restrict changes to public-facing pages, shared navigation, visual tokens, and public assets; leave the admin workflow, database, and booking behavior intact.
- Preserve each route's metadata and add missing social metadata where public pages are touched.
- Check desktop and mobile layouts, navigation, logo rendering, booking access, and the previously broken pages in a browser. Review the resulting screen for text density and repeated contact prompts.
